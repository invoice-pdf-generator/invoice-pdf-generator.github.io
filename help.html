🧾 Invoice Generator — help & how it works

A simple four-step invoice: your details, the client, the line items, done. Everything stays in your browser — nothing is sent to any server.

The four steps

  1. Your business details — name/company, address, email. Saved in your browser for next time.
  2. Client & invoice info — client name, invoice number and issue date. Use any numbering style you like (INV-001, 2026-004, …).
  3. Line items — description, quantity, unit price. The total, tax and grand total update live as you type.
  4. Notes — payment terms, bank details or a thank-you message shown below the table on the invoice.

Tip: invoice numbers are free-form on purpose — some clients want sequential numbers, others use dates or project codes. Type whatever matches your books.

Currencies & tax rates

  • Supported currencies: USD, EUR, GBP, NGN, ZAR, INR, CAD and AUD.
  • Set any tax rate percentage per invoice (e.g. 20% VAT, 7.5% GST, 0% for untaxed work).
  • Prices are entered as unit prices; quantity × unit price = line total before tax.

Save the invoice as PDF

  1. Click Preview & Print.
  2. In the print dialog, choose Save as PDF as the destination.
  3. The saved file is a clean, printable invoice you can email or archive. Works offline.

Troubleshooting

  • Totals don't add up — check you entered quantity and unit price (a quantity of 0 gives a 0 line total).
  • Tax missing — set the tax rate in step 3; 0% disables tax on the whole invoice.
  • Wrong currency symbol — change the currency dropdown before previewing; it applies to the whole invoice.
  • Invoice prints on two pages — clear long notes or reduce browser zoom before printing.
