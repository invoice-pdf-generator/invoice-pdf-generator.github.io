Frequently asked questions

Is this invoice generator really free?

Yes — 100% free, no signup, no credit card, no limits. Create unlimited invoices and print or save them as PDF right from your browser.

Does my invoice data leave my computer?

No. Everything happens locally in your browser. Your client names, prices and details are never uploaded to any server.

Can I save an invoice as a PDF?

Yes. Click Preview & Print, then choose 'Save as PDF' in the print dialog. It works offline on any device.

Which currencies and tax rates can I use?

USD, EUR, GBP, NGN, ZAR, INR, CAD and AUD, with any tax rate percentage you set per invoice.
